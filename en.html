01 / ABOUT

Solve a problem.
Shape a city

The 13×13 Innovation Competition is organized by Başakşehir Municipality and Başakşehir Living Lab.

The competition seeks innovative, practical and measurable solutions to 13 real needs in urban life. Selected projects receive training, mentoring and technical support to prepare prototypes and pilot implementations.

Competition rules (Turkish PDF) ↓Challenge topics (Turkish PDF) ↓
Is there an entry fee?

No. Entry to the 13×13 Innovation Competition is free.

Who can apply?

Students and recent graduates, researchers, public and private organizations, NGOs, startups and individuals with solutions may apply.

Can I apply alone or as a team?

Yes. You may apply as an individual, a team or an organization. Teams must appoint a representative.

Which challenge should my project address?

Choose one of the 13 published challenge areas and propose a solution that directly addresses it.

Is a proof of concept required?

To be eligible for a prize, applicants in all categories except the Open category must develop a proof of concept (PoC). A PoC is not required in the Open category.

What is the application deadline?

The application and project submission deadline stated in the rules is Friday, 15 January 2027 at 23:59.
